# LOT Research Templates

Use these templates to keep research source-linked and distinguish verified facts from reasoning.

## Evidence rules
- Add a source link for every factual claim.
- Paraphrase sources in your own words.
- Mark each finding **[Confirmed]**, **[Inference]**, or **[Check]**.
- Treat vendor licensing, tool access, and product configuration as open until confirmed directly.
